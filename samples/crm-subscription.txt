CLOUD CRM SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This Cloud CRM Subscription Agreement (the "Agreement") is made by and between Helixa Software Private Limited, a company incorporated under the laws of India ("Provider") and Brightpath Logistics LLP ("Customer").

This Agreement is effective as of 1 January 2026 (the "Effective Date").

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Subscription Services" means the hosted customer relationship management platform described in the Order Form.

1.2 "Customer Data" means all data uploaded to the Subscription Services by or on behalf of Customer.

2. TERM AND RENEWAL

2.1 The initial term of this Agreement shall be twelve (12) months from the Effective Date.

2.2 Thereafter this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive periods of twelve (12) months each unless either party gives the other written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days prior to the end of the then-current term.

2.3 Provider may terminate this Agreement for convenience at any time upon thirty (30) days written notice to Customer.

2.4 Either party may terminate this Agreement if the other party commits a material breach that remains uncured for thirty (30) days after written notice of the breach.

3. FEES AND PAYMENT

3.1 Customer shall pay a subscription fee of INR 18,00,000 per year, invoiced annually in advance.

3.2 Customer shall pay each invoice within 30 days of the invoice date.

3.3 Overdue amounts shall bear interest at the rate of 2% per month until paid.

3.4 Provider may increase the fees for any renewal term at its sole discretion by giving notice to Customer.

4. SERVICE LEVELS

4.1 Provider shall make the Subscription Services available 99.5% of the time in each calendar month.

4.2 If Provider fails to meet the availability commitment, Customer may request a service credit, and such request must be made within fifteen (15) days of the end of the affected month. Credits not requested within that period are forfeited.

5. DATA PROTECTION

5.1 Provider shall notify Customer of any personal data breach affecting Customer Data without undue delay and in any event within 72 hours of becoming aware of it.

5.2 Upon termination or expiry, Provider shall return or delete all Customer Data within thirty (30) days, at Customer's election.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY

6.1 Each party shall keep the other party's Confidential Information secret. The obligations in this clause shall survive termination of this Agreement for a period of three (3) years.

7. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

7.1 Provider's aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid by Customer in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

8. AUDIT

8.1 Customer may audit Provider's security controls and compliance records once per year on thirty (30) days prior written notice.

9. GENERAL

9.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and the courts at Hyderabad shall have exclusive jurisdiction.
