LEAVE AND LICENCE AGREEMENT

This Leave and Licence Agreement is made on 1 April 2025 between Sai Krupa Estates Private Limited (the "Licensor") and Brightpath Logistics LLP (the "Licensee").

1. Premises. The Licensor grants the Licensee a licence to use Unit 402, fourth floor, Cyber Pearl Towers, Hitec City, Hyderabad for office purposes.

2. Period. The licence is for a period of thirty-six (36) months commencing on 1 April 2025.

3. Licence fee. The Licensee shall pay a monthly licence fee of Rs. 3,20,000 on or before the fifth day of each month.

4. Escalation. The licence fee shall increase by 5% on completion of every twelve (12) months of the licence period.

5. Deposit. The Licensee has paid an interest free security deposit of Rs. 19,20,000 which the Licensor shall refund within thirty (30) days of the Licensee vacating the premises.

6. Termination. After the lock-in period of eighteen (18) months, either party may terminate this licence without cause by giving three (3) months written notice.

7. Insurance. The Licensee shall maintain insurance for its own fit-outs and contents at the premises.

8. Law. This agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and courts in Hyderabad shall have jurisdiction.
