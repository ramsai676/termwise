FACILITY MANAGEMENT SERVICES AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT is entered into on 15 January 2025 between Cleanline Facility Services Pvt Ltd (hereinafter referred to as the "Contractor") and Brightpath Logistics LLP (hereinafter referred to as the "Client").

1. Scope. The Contractor shall provide housekeeping, pantry and front desk services at the Client's Gachibowli office as described in Schedule A.

2. Term. This Agreement shall be for a period of two (2) years from 15 January 2025 and shall expire automatically at the end of that period unless renewed in writing by both parties.

3. Charges. The Client shall pay a monthly fee of Rs. 1,45,000 for the services. The Contractor shall raise invoices on the first working day of each month and the Client shall pay within fifteen (15) days of receipt of invoice.

4. Revision. The Contractor may revise the charges once every twelve months, provided that any increase shall not exceed 8% of the charges then in force.

5. Insurance. The Contractor shall maintain workmen's compensation and public liability insurance with cover of not less than Rs. 50,00,000 throughout the term.

6. Staff. During the term and for a period of twelve (12) months after its expiry, the Client shall not solicit or employ any personnel deployed by the Contractor without the prior written consent of the Contractor.

7. Termination. Either party may terminate this Agreement for any reason by giving ninety (90) days written notice to the other party.

8. Governing law. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India.
