PRINTER AND COPIER RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made on 1 March 2026 between Imagetech Office Systems Pvt Ltd ("Supplier") and Brightpath Logistics LLP ("Customer").

1. Equipment. The Supplier shall supply and maintain six multifunction printers at the Customer's Hyderabad and Vijayawada offices.

2. Term and renewal

2.1 The initial term of this Agreement is twelve (12) months from 1 March 2026.

2.2 This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive periods of twelve (12) months unless the Customer gives written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term.

3. Rental

3.1 The Customer shall pay a rental of Rs. 38,000 per month, inclusive of maintenance and toner.

3.2 Invoices are payable within twenty-one (21) days of the invoice date.

4. Service

4.1 The Supplier shall attend to any breakdown within one (1) business day. If the Supplier fails to do so, the Customer may request a service credit within ten (10) days of the breakdown.

5. Removal

5.1 On expiry or termination, the Supplier shall remove the equipment and securely erase all stored documents from the devices within fifteen (15) days.

6. Governing law

6.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of India.
